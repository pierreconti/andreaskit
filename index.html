During the decoding process of a communication, it can happen that the receiver, mistaken by his own knowledge and experience, reorganizes the message according to his own logic, and adds some new elements which were not transmitted originally but contribute to the interpretation of the message, to its re-appropriation. In the final synthesis, it is quite difficult to distinguish the added part from the original communication.

Andrea's Kit makes collages from borrowed signals and the small mistakes that survive translation, reorganizing what was already there until the seams disappear.
